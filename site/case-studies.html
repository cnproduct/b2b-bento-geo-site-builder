Supermarket Private Label Bento Box Launch (Europe)

Partner Profile: A major grocery retail chain with over 200 outlets in Germany and Austria looking to launch a premium sustainable housewares line.

The Challenge:

The client was sourcing standard plastic bento containers via trading companies, resulting in thin profit margins (under 15%) and high carbon footprint indices. They needed a direct factory partner to customize an organic, food-safe bento box with specialized leak-proof sealing, low MoQ per color, and complete FDA/LFGB safety passes.

The Solution:

Naike Gifts compounded a custom 30% organic wheat straw composite polymer for their brand. We designed a dual-compartment layout with a leak-proof silicone seal gasket, customized locking bands, and a laser-engraved logo. The first shipment cleared Xiamen Port on schedule.

The Business Result:

The private label bento line launched successfully, achieving a **25% gross margin increase** for the retailer. Over 300,000 units have been manufactured and shipped, maintaining a perfect compliance record.

Cafe Franchise Reusable Coffee Cups Sourcing (North America)

Partner Profile: A specialty coffee franchise based in Seattle with 120 locations seeking to replace single-use paper cups with sustainable travel travel mugs.

The Challenge:

The franchise required durable travel mugs that could withstand commercial dishwashers, carry heat insulation, and satisfy strict corporate carbon offset audits. Upstream factory carbon footprint verification was a critical requirement for their board's ESG metrics.

The Solution:

We supplied our double-wall wheat straw travel mug with thread-on spill-proof lids. By coupling production line energy with our 1.5 MW factory solar array and verifying that we recycle 90% of process water, we provided the client with a documented Scope 3 environmental certificate.

The Business Result:

The travel travel mugs successfully launched, offseting local waste taxes. They have become the franchise's highest-margin retail merchandise, helping them offset carbon indices across their supply chain.

School District Kids Bento Dinnerware Sourcing (Australia)

Partner Profile: A school dining distributor supplying dinnerware and bento trays to education centers across New South Wales and Victoria.

The Challenge:

The distributor was facing high breakage rates and complaints on cheap melamine tableware. They required child-safe, drop-proof compartment bento containers that complied with Disney FAMA and FDA safety standards for kids dining safety.

The Solution:

Naike engineered a drop-proof organic wheat straw compositeKids Compartment Plate. The plate features double-molded TPU shock-absorbent outer edges and child-friendly latches. All batch materials cleared strict Disney FAMA certifications before shipping from Xiamen.

The Business Result:

Field tests demonstrated a **40% reduction in dinnerware replacement rates** in schools. The distributor signed a 3-year exclusive supply contract with Naike Gifts.
