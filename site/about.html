The Ameba Philosophy: Rigorous Quality from the Inside Out

Naike operates under the Inamori Kazuo "Respect the Divine and Love People" management philosophy. Our 20,000m² facility is organized into 12 self-accounting Ameba manufacturing units—from precision mold tooling and raw material inspection to liquid silicone injection and final packaging.

Each Ameba operates with transparent daily accounting, empowering frontline technicians to treat every bento box as a work of craft. This meticulous culture explains why our defect rate remains consistently under 0.05% across millions of containers shipped annually.
