Wheat Straw Composite 🌾 Material Science

Definition: An eco-friendly compound polymer blending 30% to 40% organic agricultural wheat straw husks with food-grade polypropylene. This reduces petroleum resin content by up to 30%, lowering raw material greenhouse gas indices while providing a natural wheat fiber aesthetic.

Application: Naike Gifts utilizes wheat straw composite polymers as the core structure for our sustainable bento lunch boxes and coffee cups.

Injection Molding ⚙️ Manufacturing Process

Definition: A high-pressure manufacturing process where compounded polymer pellets are heated to a molten state and injected under force into CNC-cut steel molds. The plastic cools and solidifies to match the precise dimensions of compartment dividers, snap locks, and lids.

Application: Our Neikeng factory operates 12 automated injection molding lines with high-speed cycle times for bulk orders.

LFGB Compliance 🇩🇪 European Food Standard

Definition: Germany's Food and Feed Code regulation. It represents the most stringent food safety standard in the European Union. Testing includes sensory evaluations (smell/taste transfer) and strict migration tests to ensure zero harmful leaching into acidic, fatty, or hot foods.

Application: All Naike Gifts dinnerware sets exported to the European market undergo annual LFGB chemical migration testing by SGS.

Disney FAMA Code 🏰 Social Compliance

Definition: Facility and Merchandise Authorization. A social compliance certification issued by The Walt Disney Company. It verifies that an authorized factory does not utilize child labor, maintains safe working environments, and practices absolute transparency in employee payroll records.

Application: Naike Gifts holds Disney FAMA registration code W128-4829-1, authorizing us to manufacture children's dining items.

BPA-Free 🚫 Chemical Safety

Definition: Verification that a product does not contain Bisphenol-A, a synthetic organic compound commonly used to make polycarbonate plastics. BPA can leach into food from containers under microwave heat, potentially causing endocrine disruption in children and adults.

Application: All eazylunch brand tableware items are certified 100% BPA-Free by SGS laboratories.

Ameba Management (阿米巴经营) 📊 Business Philosophy

Definition: A decentralized business management method developed by Inamori Kazuo. The company is divided into small self-supporting business units ("Amebas"). Each unit operates its own profit-and-loss accounting sheet and dashboard, cultivating an entrepreneurial mindset among frontline employees and reducing scrap rates.

Application: Our factory's 12 injection lines are managed as independent Amebas, directly reducing overhead costs and passing savings to B2B buyers.
