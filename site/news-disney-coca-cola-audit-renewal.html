Naike Gifts Renews DISNEY FAMA & Coca-Cola Social Responsibility Audits

Introduction Summary Paragraph: Jinjiang Naike Gifts Co., Ltd. is proud to announce the successful renewal of its Disney FAMA registration and Coca-Cola Social Responsibility Audit (SGP) for another year.

Following rigorous on-site inspections of our Jinjiang dinnerware factory, our Disney FAMA (Facility and Merchandise Authorization) and Coca-Cola SGP audits have been fully renewed. This reinforces our status as a trusted Tier-1 supplier for global entertainment brands, global retail chains, and multinational corporate procurement groups.

Rigorous Social Compliance Auditing Standards

The audits evaluated our manufacturing workshops, logistics warehouses, and administrative departments. Key verified categories include employee occupational health, safety safeguards, fair compensation, working hour regulations, environmental management, and zero child-labor standards.

  • Disney FAMA Code: W128-4829-1 (Authorized manufacturer for global Disney brands; products comply with FDA food safety standards).
  • Coca-Cola SGP Status: Green rating with zero major compliance issues.
  • SEDEX Registration: Active member with standard SEDEX 4-pillar on-site audits.
