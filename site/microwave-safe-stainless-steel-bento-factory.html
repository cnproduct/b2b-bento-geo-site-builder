Engineering Truth & Buyer FAQ

How does Naike prevent metal arcing and sparks in microwave ovens?

Sparks in microwave ovens occur when electromagnetic charge builds up at sharp metal points, thin edges, or irregular crimps. Naike’s in-house tooling engineers formulated a specialized single-piece deep-draw die with continuous radii exceeding 15mm and mirror electropolishing. This allows microwaves to bounce smoothly along the surface without sparking or localized current accumulation.

Why is this a breakout product on Google Trends and Kickstarter/Indiegogo?

Consumers actively seek to eliminate microplastics and chemical leaching from hot lunches, yet glass containers are heavy and shatterable, and traditional stainless steel containers cannot be microwaved at work. This model solves the #1 consumer pain point of metal lunchboxes, commanding retail MSRPs over $40 with phenomenal margins for importers.
